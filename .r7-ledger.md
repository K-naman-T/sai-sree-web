# Round 7 ledger — spec: ROUND7.md

## Rulings

- R1: A group's photographs belong on screen together — no carousel, no click-through.
  Each group is a wall; every plate carries its own caption.
- R2: One wall system, many compositions: a 12-column grid; each group's spans/heights are
  chosen from its photos' actual ratios so tiles sit near native and crops stay mild.
  Consecutive groups must not repeat the same composition — the four 2-plate groups use
  four different treatments (staggered, mirrored, cinema, unequal pair).
- R3: The section-fit rule from round 6 still holds: wall heights are clamps derived from
  the viewport; every group section fits 100dvh - 68px at the desktop test viewport.
  The hero remains the only `data-viewport-exempt` section.
- R4: Below lg every wall is a single column of framed plates (aspect-framed, portraits
  4:5); stacking past the viewport on mobile is normal and allowed.
- R5: Reveal motion is the vendored Magic UI BlurFade (per-plate stagger, capped 0.3s).
  No new click targets — the folio is a reading surface.
- R6: Screenshot pipeline must not lie: wait for every image's decode (raced, max 4s) and
  strip framer's promoted layer styles before the full-page capture. Beyond-viewport
  capture renders such layers blank; that is a capture artifact, not a page defect (proved
  by scripts/probe_reveal.cjs: all 26 plates opacity 1, blur 0, loaded, in the settled
  page). `DEVICES=` filter added for targeted mobile/desktop runs.
