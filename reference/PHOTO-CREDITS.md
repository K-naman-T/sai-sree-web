# Photograph credits

## Site photographs (the company's own)

Twenty-six photographs of the company's own sites, supplied by the client (2026-10-10,
`consfilnal-2.zip`), now carry the Work page and the Home page Work band. They are the
client's property; no third-party licence applies and no attribution is required. They
are captioned by the work they show, kept plain and factual.

- Machine-level metadata (file, original filename, dimensions, caption):
  `assets/photos/credits.json`.
- Grouping and captions as shipped: `src/data/photos.ts`.

The earlier web stand-ins (`standin-01..06.jpg`, CC0/public-domain stock) have been
retired and removed. They were never photographs of the client's work.

## Art plates

The painted plates in `assets/brand/plates/` and derivatives in `assets/brand/web/` are
generated imagery (xAI Grok Imagine, Mysore-painting and Rangoli/Kinnala idioms per the brand
kit), treated in-house: watermark removed, derivatives cropped and resized. They are brand art,
never captioned as real sites.
