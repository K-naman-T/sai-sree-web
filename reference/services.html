Services

Sai Sree Enterprise takes on civil, earthwork, supply and machine work, and this page sets out what can be hired and how a job starts.

Civil construction contracts

We take on civil construction work as a contract. The scope follows the drawings and the specification. Foundations, columns, slabs and masonry are built on site.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Earthwork and excavation

We cut and remove earth where the drawings call for it. Excavation follows the set-out lines and the levels. The spoil is loaded and carted off the site.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Site levelling and preparation

We level and prepare ground before building starts. Pegs and string lines mark the levels. The plot is cut and filled until it is flat and ready.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Machine contracts by hour, day or trip

The hire is booked and charged by the hour, the day or the trip. The rate is agreed before the machine is booked. The booking covers the machine's time on site.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Machinery with operator

Machines are hired out with an operator. The operator runs the machine on the work in hand. The machine is chosen for the ground and the access on your site. Machine hours are written in the logbook.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Material supply (cement, steel, aggregates, fill earth)

Materials are supplied to site as the work needs them. They are brought in and unloaded on site. Supply can run with the construction or on its own.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Labour supply and labour contracts

Labour is supplied to site on contract. Teams are placed with the job for the work it needs. The team works on site for the span of the job.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Road, drain and barrier work

We build roads, drains and barriers as part of a site. Formation, drainage lines and barrier runs are set out and built to the drawings. Formation, drainage and barriers are built to the drawing levels.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Rammed earth and mud construction

Rammed earth walls are formed inside formwork. The earth mix is placed and compacted in lifts until the wall stands, then the formwork is struck. Mud construction follows the same method, and the wall is finished by hand.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

How a job starts

  1. Receive the site
  2. Survey and set out
  3. Cut and excavate
  4. Fill and level
  5. Compact and hand over
