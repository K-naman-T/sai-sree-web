About

Who we are

Sai Sree Enterprise is a construction and earthmoving company. The work runs across civil construction, earthwork and levelling, machine and material supply, and rammed earth and mud construction.

How we run

Material goes to the site. Attendance is taken at the site. Machine hours are written in the logbook. The week is checked against the diesel and the bills.
