Services

Everything we are asked to do carries the same standard: art and design at the core, from the first cut to the final finish. Here is what can be hired, and how a job starts.

Brick building under construction

01

Civil construction contracts

We take on civil construction work as a contract. The scope follows the drawings and the specification. Foundations, columns, slabs and masonry are built on site with the finish the work deserves.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Machine trenching on the plot

02

Earthwork and excavation

We cut and remove earth where the drawings call for it. Excavation follows the set-out lines and the levels. The spoil is loaded and carted off, and the cut is left true.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Land graded and set out

03

Site levelling and preparation

We level and prepare ground before building starts. Pegs and string lines mark the levels. The plot is cut and filled until it is flat and set for the build.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Clearing with the backhoe loader

04

Machine contracts by hour, day or trip

The hire is booked and charged by the hour, the day or the trip. The rate is agreed before the machine is booked. The right machine is matched to the work in hand.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Sub-base compaction inside the shed

05

Machinery with operator

Machines are hired out with an operator. The operator runs the machine on the work in hand, with the care the work needs. Machine hours are written in the logbook.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Placing concrete at the pit

06

Material supply (cement, steel, aggregates, fill earth)

Materials are supplied to site as the work needs them, chosen for the job. They are brought in and unloaded on site. Supply can run with the construction or on its own.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Laying turf on the slope

07

Labour supply and labour contracts

Labour is supplied to site on contract. Teams are placed with the job for the work it needs. The team works on site for the span of the job, and leaves the work to the standard set.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

Slope finishing and drainage

08

Road, drain and barrier work

We build roads, drains and barriers as part of a site. Formation, drainage lines and barrier runs are set out and built to the drawings. The lines are run true and the levels held.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.

09

Rammed earth and mud construction

Rammed earth is the art form we keep alive. Walls are formed inside formwork, the earth mix placed and compacted in lifts until the wall stands, then the formwork is struck. Mud construction follows the same method, and the wall is finished by hand.

What you provide: drawings, approvals, and site access.
