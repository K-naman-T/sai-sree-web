About
Art aesthetics are not decoration added at the end. They are how the work is planned and built.

Sri Sai Enterprise is a construction and earthmoving company. Art and design sit at the core of everything we build, from the first cut to the final finish.

What we do
Civil construction

Contract work built to the drawings and the specification.

Earthwork and levelling

Ground cut, filled and set to level.

Machine and material supply

Machines with an operator, and materials brought to site.

Machinery with an operator

Hired by the hour, by the day, or by the trip.

Rammed earth and mud construction

Walls formed in lifts and finished by hand.

How we run

Every site runs the same measured sequence. Craft is a discipline.

01
Receive the site
02
Survey and set out
03
Cut and excavate
04
Fill and level
05
Compact and hand over

Material goes to the site. Attendance is taken at the site. Machine hours are written in the logbook. The week is checked against the diesel and the bills.

Where

We work out of Harohalli, Karnataka.
